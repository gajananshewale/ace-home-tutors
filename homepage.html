Good to know

Questions parents ask us

Is the demo class really free?
Yes. The first demo class is completely free and with no obligation. You continue with the tutor only if you're happy with the teaching.
How do you verify tutors?
Every tutor submits a government ID and their highest qualification. Our team reviews the documents before the profile goes live with a verified badge.
Who decides the fees?
Fees are agreed directly between you and the tutor. We show an indicative range on each profile, but the final amount is yours to negotiate. We don't charge families anything.
Which areas do you cover?
We're live across Pune — Kothrud, Aundh, Baner, Viman Nagar, Wakad and more — and adding areas every month. Post your requirement and we'll tell you who's available near you.
